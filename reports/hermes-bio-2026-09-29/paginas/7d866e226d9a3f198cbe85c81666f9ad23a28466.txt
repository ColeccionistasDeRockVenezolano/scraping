instituto mexicano del sonido ims is an electronic music project created by mexico city based dj and producer camilo lara 1 along with groups like nortec collective and kinky m i s is part of a growing mexican electronica movement encouraging fusions of folk and more traditional music with modern sounds career edit lara s annual christmas compilation of the year s best tracks and early mexican institute of sound songs became a collector s item in a niche market 2 this hobby led lara to collaborate on remixes under the moniker of mexican institute of sound for bands and friends such as placebo le hammond inferno gecko turner and babasonicos in 2005 he officially founded the m i s project relying on classic mexican music samples ranging from the 1920s to the 1960s mixed with esquivel vocal samples and modern scratches beats m i s has 5 albums mejico maxico pinata soy sauce politico and disco popular 3 ep s extra extra extra suave patria ims vs sant in 2006 mexican
