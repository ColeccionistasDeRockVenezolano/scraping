band recorded two demos in 1999 later released as an album 3 the band have released eleven studio albums one ep and one compilation album the band have been praised for mixing grindcore black metal death metal industrial and other genres history edit the band was inspired by norwegian black metal bands such as mayhem burzum and darkthrone the duo recorded two demos before their first full album
