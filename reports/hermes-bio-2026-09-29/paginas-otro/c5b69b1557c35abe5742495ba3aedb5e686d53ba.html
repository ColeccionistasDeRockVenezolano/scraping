Angelica [Arista Latin] Review by AllMusic

Angelica [Arista Latin] Review by Alex Henderson

A bilingual Mexican-American pop singer who grew up in Los Angeles, Angelica is among the Latin artists who enjoyed some success in the English-language market before recording in Spanish. After having an English-language hit in 1992 with "Angel Baby" on Quality, Angelica signed with Arista Latin in 1995 and sang almost entirely in Spanish when she recorded this 1997 release. What didn't change was the teen-conscious, bubblegum nature of her approach -- on sugary tunes like "Cada Vez," "Enviandome Angeles" and "Ay Carino" and a remake of the Carpenters' 1970s hit "Superstar" (which is translated into Spanish), the commercial singer is much too cutesy and girlish for her own good. A lot of great Latin pop has successfully fused sweetness and edginess (Selena, for example), but except for the dramatic ballad "Luna En Abril," this is a CD with little bite.
