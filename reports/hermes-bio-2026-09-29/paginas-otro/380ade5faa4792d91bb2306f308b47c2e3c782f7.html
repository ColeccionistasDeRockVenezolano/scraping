Tributo a Queen: Los Grandes del Rock en Espanol Review by AllMusic

Tributo a Queen: Los Grandes del Rock en Espanol Review by Stephen Thomas Erlewine

Nothing confirms Queen's astonishing international success like the release of Tributo a Queen: Los Grandes del Rock en Espanol, a tribute record comprised entirely of Spanish-language covers of Queen songs by Spanish, Mexican, and South American bands. For many listeners, this collection is simply a novelty release, but the truth is, much of this music is surprisingly successful, even if it doesn't really compare to the original recordings. Still, anyone curious about this disc will certainly find Tributo a Queen interesting, if not necessarily satisfying.
